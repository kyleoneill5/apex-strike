How to fly (Easy mode)

• Left joystick: drag up/down to pitch, left/right to bank & turn. Release = auto-level.
• Right slider: throttle (slide up for speed). Jets take off automatically at rotation speed.
• FIRE shoots the selected weapon, WPN cycles weapons.
• Yellow button = context action: DIVE/SURFACE (sub), LAUNCH (ICBM/rocket), STAGE, GEAR.
• TILT toggles steering by tilting the phone. ❚❚ opens the menu (switch vehicle, Advanced mode).
• Keyboard: W/S pitch, A/D roll, Q/E yaw, Shift/Ctrl throttle, Space fire, X weapon, G action, C camera, T time-warp, P pause.
